My project - Rave Dice

Rave Dice is a two-player dice game where the first player to reach 50 points wins. Players take turns rolling two dice, choosing whether to keep rolling or bank their points. Play it safe or take a risk to reach 50 faster!

  • Physical dice powered by micro:bit.
  • A webpage that displays the dice results.
  • Sounds and colours inspired by a rave.
